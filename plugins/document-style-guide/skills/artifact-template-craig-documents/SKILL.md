# Document Template

Create a document from this template. Keep the reference file unchanged.

## Workflow

1. Read `artifact-template.json` and resolve its paths relative to this skill directory.
2. Load [@documents](plugin://documents@openai-primary-runtime) and invoke its reference/template workflow with the retained file.
3. Treat the user's prompt and available sources as the content input. Do not invent facts merely to fill a template slot.
4. Clone or import the reference instead of replacing its visual system with generic defaults. For charts, percentage rings, numbered explainers, callouts or quotation cards, read [Visual components](references/visual-components.md) and inspect `assets/source-preview.jpg` before authoring them.
5. Render and verify the finished document, then return the final artifact.

## Fidelity

Preserve page setup, sections, styles, lists, tables, headers, footers, and recurring page elements.

User instructions control requested content and explicit deviations. The retained reference controls layout and formatting where the user has not requested a change.

## Craig's document standard

Use this as Craig's default for new general purpose documents intended to be sent to people, including PDF requests and editable Word documents. A specifically requested design or supplied template takes precedence. Do not restyle existing documents, forms, tickets, certificates, slide decks or sensitive memorial material merely because they export to PDF. Do not apply this packaging to ordinary chat or email bodies.

### Structure

Every document in this format has a cover, a table of contents, and an introduction or context section, in that order. Cover fields are a descriptive title, optional subtitle, author and publication date. Use Craig Myles as the author, including on the cover and in footers. Use a fixed publication date, not a volatile print or system timestamp. On the cover and closing page, use an ordinal day with the month and year, for example 15th October 2026 (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th; 11th to 13th are exceptions). In footers show only the matching month and year, for example October 2026. The cover top-left carries a short document type in white, the full name and date; do not invent an organisation or logo. The introduction explains the subject, purpose and background for a reader who has not seen the conversation. Follow it with a brief summary when the content warrants one.

Adapt the remaining sections to the purpose. For example, an informational document may use background and explanation; a briefing may use findings and implications; a proposal may use approach, costs and next steps. These are examples, not compulsory sections. Recommendations, references and appendices appear only when useful. Do not assume an annual report structure from the artwork. Do not copy the reference's instructional prose, example table, sample title, October 2026 date, or generic section labels into a real deliverable.

Use short, connected paragraphs in British English and a shallow hierarchy. Cover titles and Heading 1 use Montserrat Black in capitals; smaller headings and contents entries remain in sentence case. Use I for Craig's own work. Establish context before the summary and explain reasoning before detailed recommendations. Use tables for comparisons and repeated records, not to arrange long narrative text. Do not invent facts, quotes, commitments, dates, sources or content to fill space.

### Visual layout

Use the retained Word master as the design authority. It uses A4 portrait pages, 24 mm left and right margins, 23 mm top and 22 mm bottom margins, Montserrat Regular 11 pt body type, 1.18 line spacing and 9 pt paragraph spacing. The cover uses the supplied pink and purple gradient artwork with editable text over its white area and a compact white metadata block at the top left. Cover titles are Montserrat Black 28 pt, Heading 1 is Montserrat Black 22 pt, both capitalised through Word styles; the subtitle is Montserrat Light 13 pt with normal letter spacing (w:spacing=0). Clear the inherited 0.75 pt tracking on both Subtitle and its linked character style. Heading 2 uses Montserrat SemiBold 14 pt and sentence case. Cover metadata uses Montserrat SemiBold 10 pt for Craig Myles and Montserrat Regular 10 pt for the ordinal date. Tables, captions, contents entries and closing-page details use the same Montserrat family; do not introduce Arial or another default face. Keep the underlying text in natural case for readable contents entries. Use the retained font files in assets/fonts and preserve the embedded font parts in Word. Keep text clear of the artwork; adjust the title size or cover arrangement for longer titles rather than allowing collisions. Body pages are white with dark text, purple headings (#71218A), restrained pale purple callouts, and a full-width 10 mm pink-to-purple band along the bottom edge. Within the band, white Montserrat Regular 8.5 pt text shows Craig Myles at the left and the publication month and year, such as October 2026, at the right. After a clear gap, the page number uses white Montserrat Black 14 pt, visibly larger and heavier than the footer labels. Do not include a day, abbreviated month or pipe separator in the footer. The name uses title case. Use the retained borderless footer table with vertically centred cells, rather than placing different font sizes on one text baseline. Its total width is 162 mm, with 90 mm for the author, 60 mm for the right-aligned date and 12 mm for the right-aligned dynamic page field; row height is 8 mm with zero cell padding, within the 10 mm band. The footer distance is 1.5 mm. Keep the anchoring paragraph at 1 pt with no paragraph spacing. Preserve this independent alignment when editing footer text. Preserve paragraph styles rather than simulating formatting with empty paragraphs or manual spaces. Remove inherited title borders and automatic spacing that conflict with the reference.

Keep the cover and the closing artwork page unnumbered and free of footer bands. Contents starts on physical page 2 and uses the same physical page numbering as the body. Add a separate closing artwork page with the author, document title and publication date in white; do not invent a quotation or add decorative quote marks without a real approved quotation. The sample has six pages to demonstrate the patterns; this is not a page budget. Let body text flow naturally, without a forced page break for every section. Keep headings with following text, repeat table headers and prevent avoidable row splits. Use readable captions and padding around callouts and table cells.

### Word and PDF workflow

Author an editable DOCX first, even when the requested delivery is PDF. For PDF requests, export that DOCX and deliver the PDF; include the DOCX when requested or useful to the agreed task. For Word requests, deliver the DOCX.

Use true Heading 1 and Heading 2 styles and a working table of contents. Refresh the TOC after final pagination and before export; the sample's cached entries and page numbers are not valid for a new document. A populated field cache alone is not evidence of a refresh. If the runtime cannot update Word fields, build a linked contents list from verified final PDF page locations, mark it as static in the handover, and never leave an empty field or incorrect page numbers.
