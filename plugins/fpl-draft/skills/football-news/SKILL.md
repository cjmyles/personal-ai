# Football News

Read current reporting, not remembered headlines. Prioritise significant Premier League developments and Tottenham, Scotland senior men's team and Arbroath first-team news. Include wider football only when it is a major story relevant to Craig. Do not force a story for every team. Omit a story when its people, organisation or substantive development cannot be established; do not fill News with vague placeholders. Prefer club/association/competition statements and direct manager comments; use credible reporting for context. Label reporting, rumours and inference accurately. Check publication date and event date; a newly published recap is not new news. Do not bypass paywalls.

For briefings, read the existing Runs tab through the [reporting contract](../fpl-draft/references/reporting-and-learning.md). Use `football-news:last-successful-briefing` Checked UTC as the news-check start and the briefing run start as its end. On first use, use the last successful full briefing cutoff; if unavailable, use the preceding 24 hours and say so briefly. Revisit an older story only for a material new development. Keep compact source URLs/story identities and event dates in the checkpoint's Material summary to avoid repeating syndications of the same story. Use existing headers and upsert the same row; no new tab or full-report archive. Advance only after news checking succeeds and the report is ready with required writes verified. Standalone news questions do not change briefing checkpoints unless requested.

Open the sources for shortlisted stories. Usually choose two to four, ordered by significance rather than publication time alone. Write a short editorial lead and clear compact paragraphs explaining what changed and why it matters. Link to evidence using neutral labels. On a genuinely quiet day say no significant new verified headlines; on an access or coverage failure describe the gap instead.

Keep briefing News spoiler-free even when placed first: exclude scores, winners, scorers, decisive incidents, qualification outcomes and indirect outcome hints. Do not copy spoiler-bearing headlines or link labels. Choose neutral injury, selection, managerial, administrative or fixture developments instead. Where a meaningful story cannot be explained without spoiling a completed match, omit it from News; only include relevant team implications in the final Draft section. No claims about match excitement from headline tone. A direct user request for scores or a match report may override this default for that standalone answer.

Return story, significance, dated source and evidence status to the briefing composer. Do not submit team changes, alter schedules or publish skill rules.
