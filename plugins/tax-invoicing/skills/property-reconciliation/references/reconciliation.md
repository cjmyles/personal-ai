# Reconciliation rules

## Evidence and matching

For each property and statement period, record source links and stable keys: property + agent + statement period/version; booking ID + charge type; invoice ID + line; bank transaction reference/date/amount. Correct a superseded entry rather than inserting it again. Match a receipt to a payout once only; support split or combined payments with an explicit allocation. Repeated statement lines may describe cumulative balances, not new income.

Distinguish statement evidence, independent booking/bank evidence, Craig's confirmation and estimates. Never treat a remittance advice as proof the bank received it. Historical task notes can seed a source-labelled receipt but are not a fresh bank read. Read management agreements/amendments before asserting an agreed fee; an observed percentage is not a contractual entitlement.

## Agent account

Reconcile opening balance + cash collected + credits - refunds - agent deductions - owner payouts = closing balance, using the statement's actual sign conventions. A missing/blank opening balance is not automatically zero. Match the previous closing balance and trace unresolved carry-forwards. Avoid subtracting platform/management fees twice where reported rent is already net.

Check booking dates and identifiers against the booking export or independent notifications, including cancellations, refunds, owner stays and stays spanning statement cut-offs. Track earned rent, collected cash and owner remittances separately. Explain reservations deferred by check-in/collection cut-offs and follow them into the next statement. Do not infer occupancy from a short list of paid bookings.

Check management fee rate, calculation base, GST inclusion and rounding against agreed terms. Itemise cleaning, maintenance, platform fees and other deductions with invoices; distinguish a disputed/unverified charge from a confirmed error. Recalculate displayed totals with decimal arithmetic and flag even small unexplained differences without turning them into an invented missing payment. Do not quietly adjust source values to force a balance.

Compare expected owner payouts with actual bank credits, payer aliases and references. Report exact matched/unmatched amounts and dates. Never claim the whole rental month is reconciled merely because the bank deposit equals the statement total.

## Direct property expenses and cashflow

Reconcile applicable strata, council, water/utilities, insurance, repairs and mortgage transactions to bills and receipts. Distinguish direct payments from agent-deducted expenses; cross-reference existing tax/workbook rows rather than adding duplicates. A bill or scheduled debit is not payment evidence.

Separate mortgage principal, interest and fees where source statements identify them. If unavailable, retain an unclassified mortgage cash outflow; do not call the entire payment an expense or infer deductibility. Treat deposits, transfers and capital items separately from operating income/expenses. This is cashflow reconciliation, not a tax decision.

Keep the reconciliation audit record in Notion with source links to existing authoritative bank/tax/booking records. Do not silently replace a pre-existing transaction ledger or create a competing tax total.

## Completion

Track statement arithmetic, payout matching, booking completeness, fee/deduction validation and direct-expense coverage separately. Use complete only for verified configured scope; otherwise partial with precise missing evidence. A known timing difference remains pending until its later receipt/statement treatment is verified. Report the known subtotal without presenting it as a full-period profit figure.
