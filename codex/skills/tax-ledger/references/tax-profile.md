# Tax profile

Load user-supplied facts and ledger instructions from the active rows on the configuration workbook's `Tax Profile` tab. Treat them as personal configuration, not general Australian tax advice.

## Application

1. Match the transaction's ledger date and scope to active tax-profile rows.
2. Apply the effective-date and precedence rules in `configuration.md`.
3. Follow the configured ledger instruction only within its stated scope.
4. Preserve GST shown on source evidence separately from any GST credit claimed.
5. Mark the transaction `Review` when an exact effective date, scope, or instruction is unresolved.

Do not infer residency, registration, deductibility, business use, private use, or capital treatment from unrelated evidence. Do not extend a rental-expense instruction to business expenses or another taxpayer. Recommend accountant confirmation when a configured fact is incomplete or materially affects the result.
