# Supplier rules

These rules record workflow decisions, not tax advice. Re-check source evidence when circumstances change.

| Supplier | Scope | Default category | Evidence or classification note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple iCloud | Personal business | Software and cloud storage | Use the Apple invoice or receipt and identify the iCloud subscription. Flag personal or mixed use, plan changes, credits, and refunds rather than assuming the full charge is deductible. |
| Optus | Personal business | Mobile phone | Use the bill or tax invoice. Separate or flag private use, handset or device charges, plan components, credits, and refunds. |
| BizCover | Personal business | Business insurance | Use the policy schedule and tax invoice, confirm the coverage period, and reconcile instalments as one policy series without duplicating the expense. |
| Belong | Wentworth St | Internet | Treat the service as Wentworth St even when an old Manly billing address appears. Confirm GST from the tax invoice or receipt. For post-cancellation transactions, use `No GST credit`; leave `GST shown` blank unless the source evidence states it. |
| Sydney Water | Wentworth St | Water | Match the Wentworth property or account reference. Download the original bill when available; otherwise preserve the original email. Verify GST from the bill. |
| EnergyAustralia | Wentworth St | Energy | Distinguish electricity and gas using account number and document description. Verify GST from each bill. |
| Northern Beaches Council | Wentworth St | Council rates | Detect rates notices even without a Gmail label. Verify GST treatment from the notice and flag ambiguity. |
| NSW Planning or Service NSW | Wentworth St | STRA registration | Treat an invoice, payment confirmation, and renewal notice for one payment as one transaction. Prefer the tax invoice. |
| Home contents insurer | Wentworth St | Insurance | Confirm the Wentworth St address and coverage period from the policy schedule or renewal. Distinguish contents, landlord, and building cover, and flag private or mixed-use ambiguity. |
| Strata manager | Wentworth St | Strata | Process levies and invoices; ignore operational notices with no charge. Check separately stated taxable components. |

Ignore building notices, outage notices, and meter correspondence unless they contain or support a charge.
